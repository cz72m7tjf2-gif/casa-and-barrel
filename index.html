Casa & Barrel

A modern Ghanaian home‑lifestyle studio blending curated furniture, Crate & Barrel–style kitchenware, carpets, and interior design services.

Our Vision

Casa & Barrel is a boutique home studio designed to bring modern, curated living to Ghana. Inspired by Crate & Barrel’s warmth and IKEA’s simplicity, Casa & Barrel focuses on fast‑moving homeware, curated furniture displays, and premium interior design services.

Business Model

  • Curated showroom: 3–4 sofas, dining vignette, rugs, décor.
  • Main revenue: Crate & Barrel–style kitchenware.
  • Carpets: Neutral, modern, high‑margin rugs.
  • Interior design services: Consultations, styling, on‑demand furniture import.
  • Lean inventory: No warehouse; curated displays + fast‑moving goods.

Showroom Layout (Crate & Barrel Inspired)

Entry Zone – “Welcome Moment”

  • Styled vignette: sofa + rug + coffee table.
  • Warm lighting + neutral palette.
  • Signature home scent.

Living Room Zone – “Anchor”

  • Main sofa + accent chair.
  • Coffee table + minimal décor.
  • Design consultation magnet.

Dining Zone – “Heart of the Home”

  • One dining table + 4–6 chairs.
  • Styled with Casa & Barrel kitchenware.
  • Centerpiece for visual impact.

Kitchenware Wall – “Money Maker”

  • Floor‑to‑ceiling shelving.
  • Plates, bowls, mugs, glasses, serveware.
  • Fastest‑moving inventory.

Rug & Carpet Section – “Texture Zone”

  • Vertical rug rack.
  • Neutral, modern rugs.
  • High‑margin upsell.

Design Consultation Desk – “Service Hub”

  • Mood boards + fabric samples.
  • Catalogs + digital layouts.
  • Converts walk‑ins into clients.

China Sourcing Plan

Casa & Barrel sources directly from China’s top manufacturing hubs to ensure quality and competitive pricing.

Where We Source

  • Yiwu: Kitchenware, glassware, décor.
  • Guangzhou: IKEA‑lean sofas, minimalist furniture.
  • Foshan: Dining sets, wood furniture.

Trip Plan (5–7 Days)

  • Day 1–3: Yiwu Market.
  • Day 4–5: Guangzhou furniture showrooms.
  • Day 6: Foshan factories.
  • Day 7: QC + shipping arrangements.
